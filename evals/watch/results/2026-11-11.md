## What the runs show

- The last two runs used the same prompts and differ: one passes, one misses task 005 and
  has two false alarms on task 012. The judge is near the bar, not safely over it.
- Task 005 (compressed Codex session logs) was found in 2 of the 3 runs made after long
  pieces were screened in parts, and in none before.
- The false alarms move between tasks 012 and 014 from run to run.
- Tasks 006 and 007 (expected `minor`) were missed in every run. In 006 the judge read the
  code and answered that Kitden already skips the `.trash` folder, so nothing changes. That
  answer rests on the code; the expected answer of this task may be the weaker one.
- Touched tasks bring extra findings (two to five a task). Some are real, some are noise;
  the score does not count them.
- A run takes 7 to 12 minutes and costs a few cents.
- A stronger setting does not fix the judge: Terra misses the same task (005) in every run,
  and Luna at `xhigh` costs twice as much, runs four times as long and still varies. The
  misses and the false alarms come from how the judge works, not only from the model.

The traces and the scores of every run are in Langfuse, dataset `watch-eval`.
